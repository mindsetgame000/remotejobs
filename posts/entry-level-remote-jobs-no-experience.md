Getting your first remote job is the hardest step. Employers who hire remotely get many applications, so they lean toward people who have done remote work before. The good news is that several kinds of jobs regularly hire beginners, and you can make up for missing experience with skills you can prove.

## Remote jobs that often hire beginners

- **Customer service and chat support.** Companies train you on their product; they mostly look for clear writing and patience. Chat support jobs are a good fit if you prefer writing to phone calls.
- **Data entry and admin assistant roles.** Accuracy and typing speed matter most.
- **Virtual assistant work,** especially through staffing agencies that place assistants with small businesses.
- **AI training and data annotation,** where a short assessment decides whether you are accepted.
- **Online tutoring,** if you know a subject well.

## Skills that replace experience

1. **Typing speed and accuracy.** Take our free [typing speed test](/tools/typing-speed-test/) and put your score on your resume (for example "52 WPM, 98% accuracy").
2. **Written English.** Your application itself is your writing sample, so keep it short and error-free.
3. **Common tools.** Learn the basics of Google Sheets, Zendesk or another help desk tool, and Slack. Free tutorials are enough.
4. **A reliable setup.** Mention your quiet workspace and stable internet connection.

## A 2-week plan

- **Days 1-3:** write a one-page resume focused on skills, and prepare a short cover letter template with our [cover letter generator](/tools/cover-letter-generator/).
- **Days 4-14:** apply to 5-10 real listings every day. Filter for jobs open to your country; if you live outside the US or Europe, start with work-from-anywhere jobs.
- **Every day:** follow up on applications after a week and keep a simple spreadsheet of where you applied.

## Red flags to avoid

Beginners are the main target of job scams. Never pay for training or equipment, never deposit a check from a new "employer", and be careful with interviews held only over Telegram or WhatsApp. If something feels wrong, run the message through our [job scam checker](/tools/job-scam-checker/).

"Remote" does not always mean "from anywhere". Many companies can only hire people in countries where they have a legal entity, pay payroll taxes or can meet data rules. Learning to read location requirements saves you from sending dozens of applications that will be rejected automatically.

## How to read the location line

- **"Remote, US" or "Remote (United States)"** means you must live and be allowed to work in the US.
- **"Remote, EMEA" or "Remote, LATAM"** means a region: Europe, the Middle East and Africa, or Latin America.
- **"Remote, Anywhere" or "Worldwide"** means the employer accepts applicants from any country, often as contractors.
- **Time zone rules** such as "must overlap 4 hours with US Eastern time" are common even in worldwide jobs. Check what that means for your day with our [time zone converter](/tools/time-zone-converter/).

Every job on our site shows the countries the employer accepts, so you can skip jobs you are not eligible for.

## Where people outside the US and Europe find remote work

1. **Work from anywhere jobs** at companies that hire globally through employer-of-record services.
2. **Staffing agencies** that place assistants, support agents, accountants and medical billers with US and Australian businesses. They hire heavily in the Philippines, India, Pakistan, South Africa and Latin America.
3. **Country pages.** Browse remote jobs in your own country, such as remote jobs in the Philippines or remote jobs in India, to see employers that already hire there.

## Getting paid

Global contractors are usually paid by bank transfer or through services like Wise or Payoneer. Agree on currency, pay schedule and who pays fees before you start. If an offer is in US dollars per hour, convert it to a monthly figure with the [hourly to salary calculator](/tools/hourly-to-salary-calculator/) so you can compare it with local jobs.

## Stay safe

Scammers target international job seekers with fake "worldwide" offers. Real companies interview you by video, write from their own email domain and never charge you to start. Our [job scam checker](/tools/job-scam-checker/) flags the most common warning signs.
